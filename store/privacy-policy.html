Sukoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

What Sukoon stores

Sukoon keeps your name (if you enter one), your gratitude journal entries, your dhikr counts, prayer check-ins, bookmarks, journey progress and settings. All of this is stored only on your device. Sukoon has no account system and no server. We cannot see your entries.

What Sukoon collects

Nothing. Sukoon does not collect analytics, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not send your data anywhere.

Purchases

If you buy Supporter access, the purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store and managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user id and your purchase history so your access can be restored on a new phone. RevenueCat's privacy policy applies to that data: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Microphone and Recite mode

When you tap the mic in Recite mode, your phone's speech recognition service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS) converts your voice to text. That audio is handled by your phone's provider under their policy. Sukoon only receives the text, compares it with the verse on your device, and does not store or send the audio or the text anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

Deleting your data

Open Settings inside the app and tap Reset all my data, or uninstall the app. Both remove everything.

Children

Sukoon is intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
