## Full description (Play 4000 chars, App Store 4000 chars)

Feeling lost? Far from Allah? Tired and empty even when things look fine on the outside?

Sukoon is a gentle three-minute daily companion built for young Muslims who want to come closer without pressure, guilt, or long lectures.

WHAT YOU DO EACH DAY
• Read one short verse or hadith with a simple, relatable meaning
• Write three small lines of gratitude (tap to fill, no long writing)
• Watch your light grow as your streak grows

FIND YOUR PURPOSE
A seven-step journey answers the question "Why am I here?" in plain language: Allah created you on purpose, He loves you, your purpose is to know Him and serve, and gratitude lights the way. Then build a three-action Purpose Plan and tick it off daily.

COME CLOSER, GENTLY
• Dhikr counter with soft vibration and targets
• Salah tracker with a step-by-step guide and an "it's hard today" mode
• "When I feel far away" — four small steps back home

VERSES FOR THE HEART
Short verses about hope, gratitude, purpose and peace, with Arabic, simplified meaning, slow-read mode and bookmarks.

RECITE MODE (QURAN LIVE)
Tap the mic and recite. Sukoon checks your words against the verse, marks the ones to fix in red, and shows how to say each one: the correct pronunciation, the articulation point of the letters, and the Tajweed rule to apply. Or say the meaning in English and see which ideas you carried. Recite mode measures word accuracy and teaches articulation and rules from the text. A teacher confirms your makhraj.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
Streaks, a 28-day gratitude calendar, and milestones: First Light, 7 Days of Gratitude, Heart of Shukr, Walking in Purpose.

FREE FOREVER
The daily verse, gratitude journal, dhikr counter and progress are free. Supporter access opens the full Purpose Journey, all verses, offline audio and new lessons every week, and keeps Sukoon free for someone who cannot pay.

PRIVATE
Everything stays on your phone. No account. No tracking.

Sukoon takes its name from Surah Ar-Ra'd 13:28: truly, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest.
